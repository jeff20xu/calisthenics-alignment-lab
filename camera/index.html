Limitations and camera troubleshooting

Use HTTPS (or localhost), press Start, and allow camera access. If permission is denied, enable camera access in your browser site settings, reload if needed, and press Start again. If embedded elsewhere, the host must permit camera access for this iframe. Close other apps using your camera. If no camera is available, use video upload instead.

The runtime and model require a working connection to cdn.jsdelivr.net and storage.googleapis.com. If downloading fails, check content blockers or network restrictions and try Start again. Camera frames are not uploaded to these hosts. Slow devices may process fewer than 15 frames per second; skipped frames are not queued.

Film one athlete side-on, with wrists through ankles visible. Poor light, occlusion, foreshortening, the wrong visible side, or detector orientation can prevent tracking. Missing or low-confidence joints produce gaps, not invented measurements. Inspect the overlay: even confident landmarks can be wrong. The preview is not mirrored; left/right means the athlete’s anatomical side.

All measurements are 2-D estimates, not medical advice, balance scores, or measured joint torque. Tracked posture duration is not proof of an unsupported hold: walls, assistance, and contact are not detected. The optional gravitational-moment estimate covers only a simplified static trunk-and-leg model, not net shoulder torque.

Push-up counts require sustained straight arms (at least 160°), bend (at most 100°), then straight arms again in the selected inverted posture. Partial repetitions and tracking gaps do not count as full reps. Calibration assumes an unforeshortened torso. Changing sidebar settings resets calibration, time, and counts. Hiding or leaving this page stops the camera; press Start to resume a fresh session.
